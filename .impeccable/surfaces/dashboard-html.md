# Dashboard

## Mode

Operate

## Audience and job

The shop owner scans 10–20 products and sees, without opening a chart, which marketplace is under the own shop price.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mein Shop is the reference on every card; marketplace prices are rows against it, and a red note names the undercut. The page refuses the old stacked report of one full chart per product.
OWN-WORLD: Light grey ground, white 16px cards, violet accent for the shop block, marketplace hues only on the series (Zalando orange, Otto red, Galeria blue, Amazon amber). Inter-like system sans, tabular numbers. The signature move is the shop-price block with the sparkline and the undercut sentence under the rows.
STORY: The owner reads the four KPIs, filters the grid, and opens one product to see shop versus marketplaces over time.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the radar mark, Price Radar, the subtitle, the timestamp, and Exportieren. Four KPI cards. A sticky bar with marketplace chips, status chips, sort, range, and grid or list. No search field: the owner dropped it because the catalog is about eleven products. Then a card grid: the shop photo itself, name, SKU, shop URL, Mein Shop price, sparkline, one row per marketplace with the percent gap, and the undercut note.
FORM: concept-seed key 30c63de0 (operate, surface). The approved mockup is the pinned direction and outranks that roll. Code-led: no image generation was available, and the mockup HTML is the build reference.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Light theme only. German copy and formats. Real `pricing.db` data. No heat strip and no snapshot table. Self-contained HTML.
